# Roadmap

This document outlines what's planned for future versions of AlaCraft.  
Have an idea? [Open a Feature Request](https://github.com/Ma3auka/alacraft/issues/new?template=feature_request.md) or discuss it on [Discord](https://discord.gg/QbSEPbGA8u).

---

## ✅ v2.0 — Community Platform *(released May 2026)*

- Community Portal — player posts, reactions, discussions
- 7 languages — English, Russian, Ukrainian, Spanish, French, German, Japanese
- 1,300+ block reference pages with multilingual SEO descriptions
- New tools: Nether calculator, Fireworks builder, Tellraw generator, Flat World generator
- Automated sitemap with hreflang per locale

---

## 🔜 v3.0 — Next Features *(planned)*

### Tools
- [ ] Enchantment calculator — find the best enchantments for your gear
- [ ] Potion brewing guide — full brewing tree with ingredients and effects
- [ ] XP and level calculator
- [ ] Mob drop reference — what each mob drops and at what rate

### Platform
- [ ] Mobile-first UI redesign — better experience on phones and tablets
- [ ] Dark / light theme toggle
- [ ] Player inventory sharing — share your loadout with a link
- [ ] More languages — Chinese, Portuguese, Polish

### Community
- [ ] Player ranking and activity badges
- [ ] Community recipe submissions — players suggest missing crafts
- [ ] Weekly Minecraft news digest

## 💡 Future ideas *(under consideration)*

- Public API for third-party integrations
- Minecraft server status checker
- In-game mod compatibility checker

*This roadmap reflects current plans and may change. Features are added based on community feedback and demand.*
