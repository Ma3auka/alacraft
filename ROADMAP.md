# Roadmap

This document tracks what AlaCraft has shipped and what is planned next.  
Have an idea? [Open a Feature Request](https://github.com/Ma3auka/alacraft/issues/new?template=feature_request.md) or discuss it on [Discord](https://discord.gg/QbSEPbGA8u).

---

## ✅ v2.1 — Tools Expansion *(released June–July 2026)*

A major wave of new interactive tools, content additions, and quality improvements.

**New tools**
- Craft Map — interactive graph of all crafting chains with draggable cards and Focus mode
- Paintings Catalog — all 50 Minecraft paintings with sizes, artists, and `/give` commands
- Seed Map — browser-rendered interactive biome map for any Java seed (versions 1.7–1.21.4)
- XP & Level Calculator — level ↔ experience conversion with enchanting reference
- Stack Calculator — item quantities to chests, shulker boxes, and inventories
- Create 3D Block — upload textures for all 6 faces, export transparent PNG
- Potion Generator — custom potions with `/give` NBT output
- Our Mods page — AlaLoot, AlaHorde, AlaAggro with CurseForge and GitHub links

**Content additions**
- 116 furnace and smelting recipes added to the crafts catalog
- 29 in-world interaction recipes (no crafting table required)
- Sulfur Cube guide — all 12 archetypes from Minecraft 26.2 Chaos Cubed
- Achievement Generator — Minecraft advancement-style PNG images

**Platform**
- Email verification flow improved — clear confirmation and recovery pages
- Public changelog page at `/changelog`

## ✅ v2.0 — Community Platform *(released May 2026)*

- Community Portal — player posts, reactions, shared presets
- 7 languages — English, Russian, Ukrainian, Spanish, French, German, Japanese
- 1,332+ block reference pages with multilingual SEO descriptions
- New tools: Nether Calculator, Fireworks Builder, Tellraw Editor, Flat World Generator, Stronghold Finder, Custom Crafts Creator, Seeds Catalog, Achievement Generator
- Automated sitemap with `hreflang` per locale
