# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

AlaCraft is a Minecraft community for players of all backgrounds. We are committed to making participation in this project and community a welcoming experience for everyone.

## Our standards

**Encouraged behavior:**

- Being respectful and kind to other players and contributors
- Giving constructive feedback on bug reports and feature ideas
- Helping new players find what they need
- Writing issues and comments in English so everyone can follow

**Not acceptable:**

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory comments
- Spam, self-promotion, or off-topic content in Issues or Discussions
- Publishing other people's private information
- Any conduct that would be considered inappropriate in a public community

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to GitHub Issues, Discussions, pull requests, and any other project spaces. It also applies when representing AlaCraft in public spaces (Discord, Twitter, etc.).

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported via:

- **Discord** — [discord.gg/QbSEPbGA8u](https://discord.gg/QbSEPbGA8u)
- **Twitter / X** — [@alacraftday](https://x.com/alacraftday)

The project team will review and investigate all complaints and respond appropriately. We reserve the right to remove comments, close issues, or ban users who violate this Code of Conduct.
